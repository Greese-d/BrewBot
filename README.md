# BrewBot

Name: 
BrewBot

Description: 
Fully automated barista named BrewBot - a robot making coffee ordered by customers through GUI interface. Comprised of two arms collaboting, the complex task of making a coffee is separated between them and done simulataniously, allowing greater efficiency, consistent quality and high pace of completion.
The flow of interaction is suggested as following: robot takes orders through GUI and charges the customer. Then it proceeds to fulfill the order by making a drink according to customer's selection. The drink is then served to customer through pickup window.

Menu options:
Espresso, Latte, Flat white, Iced coffee, Tea

Robot details:
Arm 1 (Milkman)- UR3e
Tasks:
Espresso - Serving drink
Latte/flat white - grabing a milk cup, filling from dispenser, frothing, pouring milk in coffee cup with espresso, putting milk cup back, serving drink
Iced coffee - filling cup with espresso with ice, filling up with milk, serving drink
Tea - put a teabag in the cup, let espressoman fill with hot water, serve drink.

Arm 2 (Espressoman)- Dobot Nova2 
Espresso - getting a cup, grinding, tamping, getting espresso, passing to milkman
Latte/Flat white - getting a cup, gridning, tamping, getting espresso, holding while milk is poured, passing to milkman
Iced coffee - getting a cup, grinding, tamping, getting espresso passing to milkman
Tea - getting a cup, pass and hold the cup while milkman puts teabag in, fill hot water, pass to milkman
